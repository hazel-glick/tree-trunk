My website is a shifting house next to a river of knowledge.

Laurel Schwulst

“A website is a file or bundle of files living on a server somewhere.”

“a website creator becomes both author and architect simultaneously”

Comparing a website to physical space can help one determine their intention behind a website and how they want it to be structured.

“when you put energy into a website, in turn the website helps form your own identity.”

“This is why websites are so important. They allow the author to create not only works (the “objects”) but also the world (the rooms, the arrangement of rooms, the architecture!).”

This makes me think back to designing my portfolio and how I was very focused on making it look nice and easy to navigate, but I like this idea of designing it with an even deeper intention that reiterates what my work is about and the personal lens that created it.

“How could a website complement what you already do rather than competing or repeating?”

“A website, or anything interactive, is inherently unfinished”

The general idea of comparing your website to whatever physical item/setting makes it easier for you to grasp it. (house, shelf, plant, garden…)

I liked the emphasis of not putting too much pressure on a website and not feeling the need to constantly update and improve it and instead follow your more natural flow of work.

A Rant About "Technology"

Ursula K. Le Guin

“technology is how a society copes with physical reality”

“Technology is the active human interface with the material world”

“One way to illustrate that most technologies are, in fact, pretty "hi," is to ask yourself of any manmade object, Do I know how to make one?”

“That's the neat thing about technologies. They're what we can learn to do.”

This quote made me wonder about AI and whether or not it surpasses this, or does it fall into this because it regurgitates things humans have done.

Interesting commentary on the vagueness of the term “technology” and how everything manmade is technology and how most of it does not receive proper credit.
